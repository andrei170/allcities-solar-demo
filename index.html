The 2026 Change

The rules changed. Most of what you will read online has not caught up.

Being the company that explains this clearly is worth more than being the company with the lowest quote — because right now almost nobody is explaining it at all.

What people still expect

“The government pays for a third of it”

This is what almost every article, every neighbour and every older quote still says. For a straightforward purchase by a homeowner, that changed at the end of 2025.

If somebody quotes you today on the assumption that it still applies, the number at the bottom of that quote may not be the number you actually pay.

What we will actually do

  • Explain which rules apply to your situation, in plain English
  • Walk through the ownership structures and what each one means for you
  • Show the real numbers for your bill and your roof, not a national average
  • Tell you to speak to your own tax adviser — because we are not one
  • Tell you if it does not pay for your house
